Our Heritage

The Story of MATCHARAP

Connecting Japanese traditional tea mastery with Filipino 'Masarap' (delicious) culinary passion.

"First harvest Uji green tea leaves whisked to vibrant perfection."

1. Direct Farm Sourcing

We source directly from heritage tea estates in Uji, Kyoto. Leaves are shade-grown for 20+ days for high l-theanine and vibrant green hue.

2. Specialty Espresso Craft

Our arabica espresso beans are roasted in small batches to complement matcha without overpowering its delicate umami notes.

3. Everyday Warmth

We aim to bring approachable luxury to everyday routines with handcrafted sips and freshly baked treats.
